Simple Process

How KhetBazar Works

01
🏛️
Admin Sets Up
Super Admin creates product categories and product master data on the platform.
02
👨‍🌾
Farmer Lists
Farmer registers, logs in, and lists products they are ready to sell with quantity and price.
03
🔍
Customer Searches
Customer searches for any product and sees all farmers who have it listed.
04
🛒
Order Placed
Order is split automatically — each product goes to the respective farmer.
05
✅
Delivered
Farmer delivers the produce and marks the order as complete. Done!
